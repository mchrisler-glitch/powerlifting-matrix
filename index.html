Powerlifting Matrix

An iPhone app that prescribes a 7-set workout from your current or goal max, based on Ken Lain's Matrix training program.

Support

Questions, bug reports, or feedback? Email mchrisler@gmail.com and I'll get back to you.

Privacy Policy

Effective August 1, 2026

Powerlifting Matrix does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party services. Everything you enter (your lifts and maxes) stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page.
